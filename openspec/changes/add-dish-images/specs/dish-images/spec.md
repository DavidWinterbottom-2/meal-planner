# Spec Delta

## Purpose

A generated picture for each distinct dinner, shown beside it in the viewer, so the week reads at a glance.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: One picture per distinct dinner

Each distinct dinner text, after normalisation, SHALL have at most one picture, reused wherever that dinner appears in any week.

#### Scenario: Same dinner in two weeks

- **WHEN** "Chicken party pies (homemade)" is planned on 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-27
- **THEN** both days show the same picture, and it is generated once

### Requirement: Generated in the background

When a week is saved or a day is updated, dinners with no picture SHALL be queued and generated by ComfyUI without delaying the save. If ComfyUI is unreachable, they SHALL stay queued and be retried. A failing dinner SHALL be retried at most 3 times.

#### Scenario: Desktop off

- **WHEN** a week is saved while ComfyUI is unreachable
- **THEN** the save succeeds immediately, and the pictures appear once ComfyUI is reachable again

### Requirement: Non-cooked dinners skipped

Dinners matching the skip list (eating out, IKEA, takeaway, leftovers, use-it-up, Kita) SHALL NOT get a picture.

#### Scenario: Dinner out

- **WHEN** Saturday's dinner is "Dinner at IKEA (out shopping)"
- **THEN** no picture is generated and the row shows none

### Requirement: Picture beside the dinner

A day row whose dinner has a finished picture SHALL show it beside the dinner, with the dinner text as its alternative text. A row with no finished picture SHALL show no picture and no placeholder.

#### Scenario: Picture ready

- **WHEN** the picture for Friday's dinner is finished
- **THEN** Friday's row shows it

### Requirement: Pictures behind the login, available offline

Pictures SHALL be served only by the viewer listener, behind the viewer login, and SHALL be saved for offline use like the pages.

#### Scenario: Offline

- **WHEN** a week with pictures was opened online and is reopened with no connection
- **THEN** the pictures show

### Requirement: Regenerate a picture

Claude SHALL be able to discard a dinner's picture and queue a new one through an MCP tool.

#### Scenario: Bad picture

- **WHEN** Claude calls `regenerate_dish_image` with "Rack of lamb + potatoes + broccoli + sweetcorn"
- **THEN** the old picture is no longer shown, and a new one appears once generated

### Requirement: Off without ComfyUI

With no ComfyUI address configured, nothing SHALL be queued or generated, and the viewer SHALL work as before.

#### Scenario: Local development

- **WHEN** the app runs without `COMFYUI_URL`
- **THEN** pages render with no pictures and no errors
